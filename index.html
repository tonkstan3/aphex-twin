Fan Lore

A name from a dream. Richard says the name "Aphex Twin" arrived to him in a dream. Over the years he's told the origin story differently every time, so fans simply stopped asking.

The bank that played his games. In the early '90s he hacked the sign of a bank in his hometown of Falmouth, flashing cryptic messages to the whole street.

Aliases without end. AFX, Polygon Window, Caustic Window, The Tuss, Power-Pill… fans have spent decades mapping his secret releases, and lost tapes still surface on auction sites for thousands.

The 13-year silence. After Drukqs (2001) he vanished. When Syro finally landed in 2014 it shipped with a handwritten note about MP3s — and nobody had even known it was coming.

Still hiding. The hunt for the location of a single grainy beach photo, the unlabelled tapes he left in record shops, the smiley that never stays still… with Aphex Twin, the lore is half the discography.
